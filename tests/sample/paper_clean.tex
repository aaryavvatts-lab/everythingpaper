\title{Sorting Widgets by Weight Reduces Errors}
\author{A. Author}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Factories sort widgets by hand. We test a weight-based sorting rule on 120 widgets and find that it lowers the error rate from 12 to 4 percent. The rule needs no new hardware.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Widget sorting matters for factories \cite{smith2020}. Hand sorting is slow and error prone \cite{jones2021}.

We test one rule: sort by weight. Our results show 12 errors per 100 widgets before the rule and 4 after (Figure~\ref{fig:overview}).

\section{Method}
We weigh each widget and sort it into one of three bins. Section~\ref{sec:method} is this section.\label{sec:method}
